# Cases are flat, with typed relations between them

A single dispute often runs through several numbered proceedings — a suit, an
appeal in a higher court, an execution petition — each with its own case number,
CINO and court. We considered grouping these under a Matter that would own the
clients, the opposite party and the assigned advocate, and rejected it: the Case
is what an advocate opens, quotes and refreshes, and putting a second aggregate
above it would mean every screen and every query had to decide which level it
was working at.

Instead each Case stands alone and holds its own diary, documents and tasks.
Continuity across proceedings is carried two ways: **Case Relations** record how
one proceeding arose from another, and **Parties** are stored once and reused, so
"every case for this client" and "every case against this opponent" are answerable
without a grouping entity.

The cost we accepted is that nothing owns the engagement as a whole. There is no
single place to record a fee arrangement or an instruction spanning the suit and
its appeal, and no one screen that presents them as one file. If that need
becomes real, the way out is to add a grouping above Cases later — not to move
the diary or documents off the Case.
